## Indexing — is the page in the index at all
Search the page's *full title* as the query. Indexed = it appears; not indexed = it does not. Do not judge indexing by keyword rank.
For long titles, cut at the first dash or comma and search again before calling it "not indexed".

## Ranking — where it shows
Rank is per query. Record the exact query, the date, and the position. A screenshot without the query string is not evidence.

## Keyword choice — niche beats volume
Pick phrases real people would type for *your* page, not the biggest monthly volume. Avoid brand names you do not own and mass-produced combinations.

## Reporting
Say what changed, from what to what, since when. Numbers without a baseline are noise.

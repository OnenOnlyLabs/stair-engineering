## Structure
Lead with the conclusion. One idea per paragraph. Cut every sentence that does not move the reader.

## Voice
Plain words. Active verbs. No hedging stacks ("it seems it might possibly").

## Length
As long as needed to be true and complete, and not one line more.

## Before publishing
Read it once as the reader who knows nothing. Fix what stops them.
